## Description

Castellan's second flagship: devices that find each other, pair by QR, and sync the vault
over the LAN with no cloud anywhere. LocalSend protocol interop means the transfer layer
talks to real LocalSend installs, not just to itself. Vault sync is delta-based with
per-entry merge and history-backed conflict resolution.
