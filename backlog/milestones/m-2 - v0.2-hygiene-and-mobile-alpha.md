## Description

The features that make the vault *safer to own* than a text file: recovery codes as a
structured type, the audit dashboard with breach checks, verified automatic backups, the
big importers, and the first Android alpha with biometric unlock. Also the point where
the extension is trustworthy enough to recommend to strangers.
